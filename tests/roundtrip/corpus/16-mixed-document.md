# Release notes

The **first** release of the editor. See <https://example.com> for the plan.

## What works

- Headings, from one to six
- Lists, both *bullet* and ordered
- Code, both `inline` and fenced

## What does not

1. Tables
2. Footnotes

> Both are out of scope for this version.
